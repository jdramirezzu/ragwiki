# The cell

Lecture 1 notes.

The cell is the smallest unit that is alive on its own. Every organism is made of one or
more cells.

## Two kinds of cells

Prokaryotic cells, such as bacteria, have no nucleus: their DNA floats in the cytoplasm.

Eukaryotic cells, such as those of plants and animals, keep their DNA inside a nucleus and
have organelles, small compartments that each do one job.

## Organelles to remember

- Nucleus: stores the DNA.
- Ribosomes: build proteins.
- Mitochondria: release the energy the cell uses.
- Chloroplasts: only in plants and algae; they capture light.

Plant cells also have a rigid cell wall outside the membrane. Animal cells do not.
