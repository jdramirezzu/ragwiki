# Cellular respiration

Lecture 2 notes.

Cellular respiration is how a cell releases the energy stored in glucose. Most of it
happens in the mitochondria.

The overall reaction uses glucose and oxygen and produces carbon dioxide, water and ATP.

ATP is the molecule cells spend when they need energy. One glucose molecule yields about
30 ATP when oxygen is available.

## Three stages

1. Glycolysis splits glucose in the cytoplasm and yields 2 ATP.
2. The Krebs cycle runs inside the mitochondria and releases carbon dioxide.
3. The electron transport chain, on the inner mitochondrial membrane, makes most of the ATP
   and needs oxygen.

Without oxygen the cell stops after glycolysis and ferments, which yields far less energy.
