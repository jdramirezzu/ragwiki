# Photosynthesis

Lecture 3 notes.

Photosynthesis is how plants, algae and some bacteria turn light into chemical energy. In
plants it happens in the chloroplasts.

It uses carbon dioxide, water and light, and produces glucose and oxygen.

## Two stages

The light reactions happen in the thylakoid membranes. They split water, release oxygen and
store energy in ATP and NADPH.

The Calvin cycle happens in the stroma. It uses that ATP and NADPH to build glucose from
carbon dioxide.

Chlorophyll, the pigment that absorbs the light, reflects green, which is why leaves look
green.
