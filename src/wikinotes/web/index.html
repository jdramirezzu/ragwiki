    How to read these results
    • Answered counts answers with at least one valid citation. It is not correctness: expected answers and your verdicts measure quality.
    • The wiki pays once to write its pages and then spends more per question, because it reads them. RAG has no building cost and makes one call per question.
    • In the study behind wikinotes (100 arXiv papers, 100 questions, the same model for both arms) the wiki answered 0.70 of the answerable questions and RAG 0.48 with 5 retrieved paragraphs, at 7.2× the cost per question. With 20 paragraphs the gap fell to +0.06 and was no longer significant. Try more than one value of k.
